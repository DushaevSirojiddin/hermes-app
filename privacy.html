Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Hermes Personal ("the app") is a single-user tool operated by Sirojiddin Dushaev for his own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app reads and writes the owner's Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and contacts via Google APIs.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests (for example, reading a spreadsheet or sending an email). It is processed on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties and is not used for advertising. Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
